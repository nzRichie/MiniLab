Brindle Logistics -- returns and claims policy

1. A claim for a damaged or missing consignment must reach the depot that
   raised the delivery note within 14 days of the delivery date printed on it.

2. A consignment refused at the door returns to its origin depot at the
   sender's cost. The return leg is charged at the same tariff band as the
   outbound leg.

3. Pallet exchange is settled at the point of delivery. A pallet not exchanged
   at the door is invoiced at the rate in the current tariff.
